Job Market Paper

Dams and Rural Labor Markets in India

Large dams deliver irrigation and power across wide regions, while their reservoirs submerge land in the villages that host them. This paper examines how the labor markets of these host villages adjust to reservoir formation in India. Non-farm employment and irrigated area grow no faster near the reservoir, so the composition of work shifts toward farm wage labor, the lowest-paid form of rural wage work, rather than out of farming.
