Job Market Paper

Dams and Rural Labor Markets in India

Does large-scale infrastructure reverse structural transformation in the places that host it? Combining India’s national registry of large dams with a three-round census panel of surrounding villages, I show that where dams are completed, local work shifts back toward agricultural wage labor while non-farm employment contracts — the opposite of India’s national trend over the same decades.
